## About Us

**XorraBet aims to be the leader in "autonomous DeFi gambling" where machines not only facilitate but actively participate in the economy of bets and trades. The platform will blend autonomous agentic cryptocurrency trading and sports betting, with popular user-centric betting options like casino games, poker, bingo, and slots. Integrating x402 protocol for faster transactions and much lower fees, XorraBet will be able to attract both global crypto enthusiasts and crack open the \$100B+ online sports betting market to mainstream adoption of crypto.**
